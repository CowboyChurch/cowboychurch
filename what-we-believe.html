What We Believe

Statement of Faith

These beliefs summarize the foundation of faith and ministry at Cowboy Church.

The Bible

We believe the Bible to be the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God and the final authority in all matters of faith, doctrine, and conduct.

God

We believe there is one true God eternally existing in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man, born of a virgin, lived a sinless life, died as the atoning sacrifice for sin, rose bodily from the dead, ascended into Heaven, and will personally return again.

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit convicts of sin, regenerates believers, indwells Christians, empowers ministry, and produces spiritual growth.

Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace alone through faith alone in Jesus Christ alone.

The Church

We believe the Church is the Body of Christ and exists for worship, discipleship, fellowship, ministry, and evangelism.

Ordinances

The Church recognizes believer’s baptism and the Lord’s Supper as ordinances established by Jesus Christ.

Last Things

We believe in the personal return of Jesus Christ, the resurrection of the dead, and final judgment.

Cowboy Church

A place to belong… where everyone feels at home. Come as you are… leave changed!
