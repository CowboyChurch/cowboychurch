Families Welcome

Children & Youth

A safe, welcoming place for children, students, and families to grow in faith.

Safety first. Jesus at the center.

Our children and youth ministries are designed to help young people feel welcomed, cared for, and encouraged as they learn about Jesus.

We are continuing to build this page with ministry-specific details, but the heart is simple: families should know their children are valued, protected, and invited to grow in faith.

Questions? Contact Kayla Christensen, Office Manager and Children's Director, at info@cowboychurch.org.

What families can expect

Welcoming leaders

Children and students are cared for by people who want them to feel seen, safe, and loved.

Clear communication

Parents are invited to ask questions and stay informed about children and youth activities.

Faith formation

Our goal is to help young people know Jesus, understand Scripture, and walk in faith.
