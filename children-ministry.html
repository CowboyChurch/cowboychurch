Children's Ministry

A safe, welcoming place for children, students, and families to grow in faith.

Jesus at the center.

Our children's ministry is designed to help kids feel welcomed, cared for, and encouraged as they learn about Jesus.

The heart is simple: children are valued, protected, and invited to grow in faith.

Questions? Contact the church at info@cowboychurch.org.

What children can expect

Welcoming leaders

Children are cared for by people who want them to feel seen, safe, and loved. All leaders have completed background checks on file.

Clear communication

Parents are invited to ask questions and stay informed about children's ministry activities.

Our Vision

For All Kids to meet Jesus, to know Jesus, to receive Jesus, to grow with Jesus, to share Jesus.
