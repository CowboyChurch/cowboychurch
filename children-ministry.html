Kids Ministry

A safe, welcoming place for kids, students, and families to grow in faith.

Jesus at the center.

Our kids ministry is designed to help kids feel welcomed, cared for, and encouraged as they learn about Jesus.

Questions? Contact the church at info@cowboychurch.org.

What kids can expect

Welcoming leaders

Kids are cared for by people who want them to feel seen, safe, and loved. All leaders have completed background checks on file.

Clear communication

Parents are invited to ask questions and stay informed about kids ministry activities.

Our Vision

For all kids to meet Jesus, to know Jesus, to receive Jesus, to grow with Jesus, to share Jesus.
