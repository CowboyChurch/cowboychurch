Youth Ministry

A place for students to grow in faith, build friendships, ask questions, and follow Jesus together.

For middle school and high school students.

Rooted in Christ. Growing in Faith. Living on Mission.

Cowboy Church Youth Ministry exists to help students know Jesus, grow in faith, and build meaningful relationships with other students and trusted leaders.

Students are invited into a welcoming environment where they can ask honest questions, learn from God's Word, and discover how faith applies to everyday life.

Youth Ministry is led by Pastor Jeremiah.

Teaching Series

Foundations of Faith

Students grow through biblical teaching that builds a strong foundation for everyday life and following Jesus.
