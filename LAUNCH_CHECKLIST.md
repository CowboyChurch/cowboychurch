## Kayla admin training test

1. Log in at `/admin/`.
2. Open **Church Calendar**.
3. Add a test event with title, date, time, location, recurring schedule, and description.
4. Publish.
5. Verify the event appears on `events.html` and `calendar.html`.
6. Open **Stories & Photos**.
7. Add a test story with title, date, image, summary, and story body.
8. Publish.
9. Verify the story appears on `stories.html`.
10. Delete the test entries or rename them for launch.
