Our Church Family

Who We Are

A warm explanation of the church family, leadership, culture, and purpose.

“From the moment I stepped into the church, I knew I was home.” — Polly

No matter where you’re coming from, you are welcome here.

We are a Christian, non-denominational, Bible-based church with a distinct western style that welcomes both country and city folks alike. Our atmosphere is warm and inviting, and the gospel of Jesus is shared in a simple, straightforward way that connects to everyday life.

Purpose, Mission & Vision

Purpose

Cowboy Church exists to introduce people to a life-changing relationship with Jesus, become fully devoted followers of Him, and equip them to lead others on the same journey.

Mission

We love God, love people, and help people discover, grow in, and share the hope of a relationship with Jesus.

Vision

To see lives, families, and communities transformed by Jesus as people become fully devoted followers who help others experience the same life-changing relationship with Him.

Leadership

Meet the pastors and ministry leaders serving The Cowboy Church family.

RP

Rick Peterson

Lead Pastor

Pastor P has a diverse vocational background including pastoral ministry, executive administration, medical operations, athletics, and coaching. He relaxes by training, competing, and coaching others in distance races and marathons. Rick and his wife Julie have two adult children and one grandchild.

KC

Kayla Christensen

Administration & Operations Manager | Children's Ministry Director

If you've spent any time around The Cowboy Church, chances are we've crossed paths! Whether I'm helping things run behind the scenes, planning the next ministry event, organizing details around the church, or hanging out with the kids on a Sunday morning, I love being part of what God is doing here.

I've been involved in Children's Ministry for more than 35 years, and I still get excited every time I have the opportunity to share Jesus with children. Watching kids discover God's love never gets old! Vacation Bible School is one of my favorite times of the year.

Today I have the privilege of serving full-time as the Administration & Operations Manager and Children's Ministry Director, and I truly love using the gifts He's given me to serve our church family. Outside of church, my greatest joy is spending time with my husband of 44 years, our three children and their spouses, and our eight incredible grandchildren.

Above all, my life is centered on Jesus Christ. His love, grace, and faithfulness have carried me through every season of life. Serving Him—and serving others—is one of the greatest blessings I could ever ask for.

JW

Jeremiah Willard

Associate Pastor

Jeremiah Willard serves as the Associate Pastor with the Cowboy Church. His dedication to helping people find purpose through Jesus Christ is his passion, and a calling for discipleship.

He is currently enrolled at Bethel University working towards his MDiv degree. Before his calling into ministry, he was in the IT field for 15 years.

In his down time he enjoys spending time with family, fishing, and hunting. Jeremiah and his wife Ruth Ann have 3 adult children, 2 grade school children, and one grandson with another on the way.
