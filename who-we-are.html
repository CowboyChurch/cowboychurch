Who We Are

“From the moment I stepped into the church, I knew I was home.” — Polly

No matter where you’re coming from, you are welcome here.

We are a non-denominational, Bible-based church with a distinct western style that welcomes both country and city people alike. Our atmosphere is warm and inviting, and the message of God’s love is shared in a simple, straightforward way that connects to everyday life.

Purpose, Mission & Vision

Purpose

Cowboy Church exists to introduce people to a life-changing relationship with Jesus, become fully devoted followers of Him, and equip them to lead others on the same journey.

Mission

We love God, love people, and help people discover, grow in, and share the hope of a relationship with Jesus.

Vision

To see lives, families, and communities transformed by Jesus as people become fully devoted followers who help others experience the same life-changing relationship with Him.

Cowboy Church

A place to belong… where everyone feels at home. Come as you are… leave changed!
