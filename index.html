Welcome Home

Warm, relaxed, Bible-based, and easy to walk into.

People often say Cowboy Church is one of the warmest, most welcoming churches they’ve ever attended, and we believe that’s exactly how church should feel. Here, you’ll find a relaxed, come-as-you-are atmosphere where you can settle in, take a deep breath, and feel right at home.

More than anything, this is a place to connect with people who love Jesus and one another and will walk alongside you through both the good times and the hard seasons.

Take your next step.

Our heart is to grow in our knowledge of Jesus while sharing His message of love, grace, and forgiveness. It’s a message that truly changes lives.
