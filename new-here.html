New Here

Clear next steps for first-time guests: service time, what to expect, kids, coffee, worship, and fellowship.

What to expect

Our Sunday service begins at 10:00 a.m. with an optional line dance and wraps up about 65–70 minutes later with a joyful rendition of “Happy Trails.”

Dress comfortably; cowboy hats, baseball caps, boots, jeans, t-shirts, or no hat at all are all welcome.

Come early and stop by our General Store for Christian gifts, apparel, and accessories or grab some free coffee and treats from our amazing Cowboy Cafe.

Sunday rhythm

Before 10:00 AMCome early, grab coffee and treats, visit the General Store, and settle in.
10:00 AMSunday worship begins with a relaxed, welcoming atmosphere and country-style worship.
Third SundaysCommunion is celebrated, followed by the Hungry Cowboy Meal after service.
After ServiceStick around, meet people, ask questions, and let us know how we can pray for you.
