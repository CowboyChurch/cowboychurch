What to Expect

Our Sunday service begins at 10:00 a.m. with an optional line dance and dress comfortably; cowboy hats, baseball caps, boots, jeans, t-shirts, or no hat at all are welcome.

Come early and grab some free coffee and treats from our amazing Cafe.

Sunday Rhythm

Before 10:00 AMCome early, grab coffee and treats, and settle in.
10:00 AMSunday worship begins with a relaxed, welcoming atmosphere and country-style worship.

Kids Pre-K through 5th Grade are dismissed after worship music to head to Kids Church.
After ServiceStick around, meet people, ask questions, and let us know how we can pray for you.
