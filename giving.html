Generosity & Stewardship

Giving

Give online, during worship, or by mail as an act of worship, gratitude, and trust.

“But who am I, and who are my people, that we should be able to give as generously as this? Everything comes from You, and we have given You only what comes from Your hand.”
— 1 Chronicles 29:14

Giving is worship.

At Cowboy Church, we believe that everything we have ultimately belongs to God. Financial giving is an act of worship, gratitude, and trust.

Through our giving, we participate in God's work of changing lives, strengthening families, serving our community, and sharing the hope of a relationship with Jesus Christ.

Give Online

Three ways to give

Online

Give securely through Online Giving using Venmo, PayPal, Apple Pay, or credit/debit card.

Worship Service

Give cash or check during the worship service offering.

Mail

Mail donations or tithes to Cowboy Church, 17525 Central Ave NE, Ham Lake, MN 55304.

Stewardship

Our time, abilities, relationships, finances, and opportunities are gifts entrusted to us by God. Stewardship is the faithful management of those gifts in a way that honors God and advances His Kingdom.
